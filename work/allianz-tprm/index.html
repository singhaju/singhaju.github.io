AI agents for third-party risk at Allianz Ayudhya

Two AI-agent prototypes for third-party risk management, built in the Information Security team.

Context

Information security internship at Allianz Ayudhya (4 June to 31 July 2026, university summer training). I was mentored by the Chief Information Security Officer, Apichai Wongchindakhun, and worked with the Information Security team. I spent the first weeks learning the team's manual third-party risk management (TPRM) process, from the supplier side and from the buyer side.

Outcome

  • Questionnaire agent demonstrated, 6 Jul 2026
  • Both tested on real, anonymized cases
  • Final presentation of both, 31 Jul 2026

Prototypes built in a two-month internship. The screens are internal to Allianz Ayudhya, so none are shown.

The problem

Allianz Ayudhya sits on both sides of third-party risk. As a supplier, the Information Security team fills in long due-diligence questionnaires by hand, searching internal documents for every answer. As a buyer, business owners who rarely do it must run the risk process for each new vendor.

Agent 1: questionnaires (supplier side)

It reads the company's internal policy and evidence documents, matches each questionnaire question to the evidence, and drafts the answer. Every answer comes with the evidence it used, a reason and a confidence score. A gap analysis lists the questions the evidence cannot fully answer, and a fit-gap analysis compares current controls with what the questionnaire asks for.

Agent 2: guidance (buyer side)

A guided assistant for business owners who are new to the process. It explains each step and the key ideas, keeps track of where the owner is, checks their inputs and outputs at each step, and warns about anything that needs a careful review, so mistakes are caught early.

How I worked

Requirements with the team first, then the design (reading the documents, the prompts and the reasoning flow), the build, and tests against real, anonymized cases. I showed my progress to the team often and refined both agents from their feedback. I demonstrated the questionnaire agent on 6 July 2026; after end-to-end tests and documentation, I presented both agents on 31 July 2026.
