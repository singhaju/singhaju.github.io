Context

Information security internship at Allianz Ayudhya (June to July 2026, university summer training). I was mentored by the Chief Information Security Officer, Apichai Wongchindakhun, and worked with the Information Security team.

The problem

In third-party risk management, a supplier answers a due-diligence questionnaire and a business owner has to assess the answers.

What I built

Two AI-agent prototypes for third-party risk management. The supplier-side agent answers due-diligence questionnaires; each answer comes with evidence, reasoning and a confidence score, and it flags gaps. The buyer-side agent guides business owners through the assessment.

Outcome

  • Two prototypes, demonstrated on 6 Jul 2026
  • Final presentation, 31 Jul 2026

Prototypes built during a two-month internship.
